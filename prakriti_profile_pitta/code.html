Your Balance Zone

Cooling & Restorative

Because your internal fire burns bright, you must actively seek cooling environments, foods, and practices to prevent burnout and irritability.

Prioritize sweet, bitter, and astringent tastes. Engage in activities near water or in nature during the cooler parts of the day. Avoid excessive competition and allow yourself unstructured time. Cultivating a mindset of surrender and playfulness is crucial to maintaining your dynamic equilibrium.

Yoga Connection

Moderation & Flow

Your practice should focus on releasing heat and tension rather than building more of it. Embrace fluidity.

Favor Moon Salutations over intense Sun Salutations. Incorporate forward folds, twists to massage the digestive organs, and cooling pranayama like Sheetali (cooling breath). Avoid practicing in hot rooms or pushing past 80% of your physical capacity. Let your practice be a moving meditation of surrender.
